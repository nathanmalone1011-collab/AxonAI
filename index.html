Questions

Things owners usually ask

Do I need to change my phone number or software?

No. Axon AI connects to the way you already operate — your existing phone number, your existing scheduling tools. Nothing changes for your customers except that someone always responds.

What if I already use ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro?

We integrate with all of them. Axon AI handles the automated follow-up, review requests, and lead nurture that those platforms don't do natively.

How long until it's actually live?

Typically two weeks from your first call to a fully working system. You'll get a short walkthrough before it goes live, and we test everything before your customers ever see it.

Is this a call center answering my phone?

No. This is automation, not outsourced labor. The system texts back, follows up, and books appointments — it doesn't pretend to be a person, and your team is always looped in on anything that needs a real conversation.

What happens if I want to cancel?

Month-to-month means no long-term contract. 30 days' notice and you're out — no cancellation fees, no hard feelings.
